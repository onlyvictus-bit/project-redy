# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Triad Workbench to be a respectful, constructive, and practical project space.

Examples of expected behavior:

- be respectful and direct
- assume good intent
- give actionable technical feedback
- discuss code and product choices without personal attacks
- help new contributors understand context when possible

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment or intimidation
- personal insults
- discriminatory language
- hostile pile-ons
- publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable and safe.
